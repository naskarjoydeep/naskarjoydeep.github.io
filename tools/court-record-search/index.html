Court Record Search

Federal dockets and published opinions by party name, with name disambiguation. Everything runs in your browser — there is no server.

This is not a background check. It cannot be used for decisions about employment, housing, credit or insurance. US federal law (FCRA, 15 U.S.C. §1681) routes those through a consumer reporting agency with the subject's consent and an adverse action notice. This page is not one and cannot be made into one.

Read before acting on any of this

A name is not an identifier. A search for one name returns records belonging to several different people. Open the linked filing and confirm identity from its contents before attributing anything to anyone.

A filing is an allegation. A docket entry records that something was claimed, not that it was true or that it succeeded. Read the disposition.

Groups are hypotheses. They are built from shared attorneys, co-parties and name forms — not verified identity. The grouping is tuned to split rather than merge, because wrongly merging two people means attributing a stranger's record to your subject. “Unlinked” means no evidence, never “different person”.

Absence of a match is not a clean record. RECAP is a partial mirror of PACER, and most state trial courts — where the majority of US prosecutions happen — are not indexed at all.
