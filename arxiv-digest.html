arXiv Margins

A daily automated read of new arXiv papers in holography, quantum information, and mathematical physics — a plain summary plus a first-pass AI referee note for each.

Generated automatically every day: new arXiv papers are matched against a topic list, then read and annotated by Claude. Treat the notes in the margin as a fast, sometimes-wrong first pass — not a substitute for reading the paper.
